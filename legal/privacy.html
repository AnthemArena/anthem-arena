Privacy Policy

What the site collects, what is public and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated 16 October 2026

In plain English

There are no ads and no analytics on the site. You are signed in anonymously, so you can vote without giving your name or email. We keep what the site needs to run the cup and your profile, we do not sell it, and you can ask us to delete it.

Who we are

Arcane Moments is a small, fan-made project run by an individual. To reach us about anything on this page, email us.

What we collect

We only collect what the site needs to work:

  • An anonymous ID. When you visit, the site signs you in anonymously (Google Firebase Authentication). That creates a random ID with no name or email attached.
  • Your votes. Votes are saved against that ID so we can stop duplicate votes, count results, and show you your own votes.
  • Your profile. A username, avatar and companion you choose, plus levels, XP, streaks and achievements the site calculates from what you do, and your settings.
  • Community activity. Posts, comments, likes and reactions you make, and activity items such as "voted in a match", saved with your username.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we see your email address and what you write.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and database providers see technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, as any website does.

What is public

Your username, avatar and level, and your posts, comments, reactions and the activity items above, may be shown to other visitors on the feed, activity and profile pages. Your anonymous ID is not shown. Please do not put your real name or other personal details in your username, avatar or posts.

What stays on your device

The site keeps small items in your browser's storage so it works without asking you to sign up again: for example your anonymous ID, username, avatar, chosen companion, which alerts you have seen, and your recent votes. We do not set our own cookies for tracking. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings, but you may lose your profile on that device.

Services we use

Some of what you see comes from other companies, who may receive your IP address and device details when your browser loads their content, and who have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase for anonymous sign-in and the database that stores votes, profiles and posts
  • Netlify to host the site and run its servers
  • YouTube for the embedded edit players and edit thumbnails (it may set cookies when a player loads)
  • Riot Games for champion images used in avatars and companions
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs for fonts and icons
  • Ko-fi and Amazon only if you click a link to them

How we use it

To run the cups and show results, to give you a profile and community features, to spot and stop cheating and abuse, to answer you when you write, and to keep the site working. We do not sell your information, do not use it for advertising, and do not use analytics or tracking services at the moment. If that changes we will update this page first.

How long we keep it

We keep votes and profiles while the site runs, so past cups and your profile stay available. Vote totals are part of the permanent record of a cup.

Your choices and rights

You can browse and vote without giving us any personal details. You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete your profile, posts and comments; email us with your username so we can find it. If we delete your profile, votes already counted remain in the cup totals but are no longer linked to a profile. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local data protection law, and you may complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us any, please tell us and we will remove it.

Security and where data is stored

We rely on our providers' security and take reasonable care, but no service is completely secure. Our providers run servers in several countries, including the United States, so your information may be processed outside your own country.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above shows when. We will say so on the site if a change is significant.
