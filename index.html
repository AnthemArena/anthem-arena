How it works

  1. Edits enter. Fan edits of Arcane, made by creators on YouTube, are drawn into groups of four.
  2. You vote. Every match is two edits head to head. Watch both, then pick your favourite.
  3. Two go through. The top two in each group move on to the knockout.
  4. One is crowned. Knockout rounds run until a single edit is left standing.
