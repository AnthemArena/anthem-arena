What it is

Arcane Moments is a fan project that celebrates the edits the community makes of Arcane. Fans vote on short head-to-head matches, and the edits that win the most votes move on until one is left standing.

Every edit is made by a fan. We show it with the creator's name and a link to their channel, and the video plays from YouTube, so the views and likes go to them.

How a cup works

  1. Edits enter

    A set of fan edits is chosen for the cup. You can see all of them in the gallery.

  2. The draw

    Edits are ranked by views and likes into four pots, and each group of four gets one edit from every pot. The strongest edits can't knock each other out in the groups.

  3. You vote

    Each match is two edits side by side. Watch both and vote for the one you like more. You get one vote per match, and the live split appears once you have voted.

  4. Top two go through

    In each group, the two edits with the most wins advance. If wins are level, the share of the votes decides.

  5. Knockout to champion

    From there it's single elimination: win and you go on, lose and your run ends. The last edit standing is the champion.

Fair play

  • One vote per match. It is enforced by the database rules, not just by the page.
  • Public counts. Vote totals for every match are shown on the bracket, so anyone can check them.
  • The draw is by numbers. Seeding comes from views and likes, then the groups are drawn automatically.
  • Honest limit. No public vote can stop every determined attempt to game it. If you see something that looks wrong, tell us.

Your companion

Pick an Arcane character in your settings and they will react to your votes, your achievements and your level-ups, in their own voice.

    For creators

    We do not host, copy or re-upload any video. Every edit is embedded straight from YouTube, so it plays from your own channel and the views and likes go to you. If you delete it, make it private or turn off embedding, it stops playing here. Each edit is shown with your name and a link to your channel.

    If you would rather your edit was not shown on the site or entered in a tournament, email us with a link to your video and we will remove it, including its title and your name from our pages. Removal is quickest before a cup is drawn.

    Get involved
