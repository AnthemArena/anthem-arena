# Caitlyn voice review

**Voice:** formal, precise, dry and measured. Procedural/forensic vocabulary used lightly. Duty and fairness. Honest about setbacks, rare brief warmth. No slang, caps, emoji, or nicknames for the viewer. Patterns only; no show lines copied.

Sections rewritten: all alerts (plus new `lowvotes`, `encouragement`), all 35 achievements, new `bubble`, `modal`, `pages`, `share`.

## Sample lines

- alerts/danger: {momentTitle} is down {voteDiff}. That is a heavy deficit, but a deficit is not a verdict. It can still be contested.
- alerts/nailbiter: Level. One vote decides it, and it has not been cast yet.
- alerts/winning: {momentTitle} holds {userPct}%. That is not a lead, it is a conclusion.
- alerts/comeback: {momentTitle} was down {voteDiff} and has taken the lead. That is a remarkable reversal.
- alerts/ally: {username} voted for {momentTitle} as well. Sound judgment on their part.
- alerts/rival: {username} voted for {theirMoment} against {yourMoment}. We disagree, and that is permitted.
- alerts/closing-24h: {matchTitle} closes in {timeLeft}, and your vote is not yet on the record.
- alerts/novotes: No votes on {matchTitle}. An untouched case.
- alerts/match-won: {momentTitle} advances. A sound result.
- alerts/match-lost: {momentTitle} did not advance. That is disappointing.
- achievements/first-choice: Your first vote. Everything begins with one.
- achievements/night-owl: Voting at this hour. I understand. Some thinking is best done at night.
- achievements/level-up: A new rank. Well earned.
- achievements/default: Achievement unlocked: {name}. Well done.
- bubble/voted-behind: You're behind. That isn't a reason to retreat.
- bubble/closed-won: Your pick won. Your judgment was sound.
- modal/first-vote: Your first vote. Welcome. Every record starts somewhere.
- pages/live-fresh: {unvoted} matches and you haven't chosen a side yet. Start with whichever you have an opinion on.
- share/invite: {a} or {b}. One vote decides it. Choose carefully.
- share/nailbiter: {pick} and {other} are {voteDiff} votes apart. Every vote matters now.
- share/losing-bad: {pick} is behind {other} by {voteDiff}. It needs support.
- share/dominating: {pick} is ahead of {other} at {pct}%. Come and see why.
