# Vi voice review

**Voice:** blunt, tough, loyal, short sentences. Gruff humour, protective, no flowery talk. No emoji, caps or nicknames for the viewer. Patterns only; no show lines copied.

Sections rewritten: all alerts (incl. `lowvotes`, `welcome.encouragement` plus a top-level `encouragement` copy), all 35 achievements, new `bubble`, `modal`, `pages`, `share`.

## Sample lines

- alerts/danger: {momentTitle} is down {voteDiff}. That's a hole. We've climbed out of worse.
- alerts/nailbiter: Dead level. One vote swings it. Find that vote.
- alerts/winning: {momentTitle} is on {userPct}%. That's a beating.
- alerts/comeback: {momentTitle} was down {voteDiff} and just took the lead. Told you.
- alerts/ally: {username} voted with you on {momentTitle}. Good call, both of you.
- alerts/rival: {username} voted for {theirMoment} against {yourMoment}. Bad call.
- alerts/closing-24h: {matchTitle} closes in {timeLeft} and you haven't voted. Get on it.
- alerts/novotes: No votes on {matchTitle}. Somebody go first.
- alerts/match-won: {momentTitle} won. Told you.
- alerts/match-lost: {momentTitle} lost. That stings.
- achievements/first-choice: First vote. See? Not hard.
- achievements/night-owl: Voting at this hour? Get some sleep. Then come back.
- achievements/level-up: New rank. You earned it.
- achievements/default: Achievement unlocked: {name}. Nice one.
- bubble/voted-behind: You're behind. Doesn't mean you're wrong.
- bubble/closed-won: Your pick won. Good call.
- modal/first-vote: First vote. Welcome. Everyone starts somewhere.
- pages/live-fresh: {unvoted} matches and you haven't picked a side. Start with the one you've got an opinion on.
- share/invite: {a} or {b}. One vote decides it. Pick.
- share/nailbiter: {pick} and {other} are {voteDiff} votes apart. Every vote counts now.
- share/losing-bad: {pick} is behind {other} by {voteDiff}. It needs help.
- share/dominating: {pick} is ahead of {other} at {pct}%. Come see why.
